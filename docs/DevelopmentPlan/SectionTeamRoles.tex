Each team member should embrace all roles present, not leaving the responsibility of each of these roles entirely on one person, however their main focus should be on the roles assigned to them.

Meeting Roles:
Chair - Generally runs the meetings, mostly creating the agenda(everyone should help) and keeping the team on track to cover all items in the meeting
Notetaker - Records everything discussed, updating tasks, deadlines, and next steps/action items on the GitHub
Progress Tracker - Follows up on details from previous meetings and deadlines to ensure team stays on track with older tasks
Quality/Scope Assessor - Assesses the scope of each decision made and ensures the team isn’t biting off more than they can chew
Risk Assessor - Should question all assumptions made and attempt to poke holes in all decisions, should try and get team to think deeply about everything (devil’s advocate)
Retrospective lead - Review what we have been doing and figure out what is and isn’t working for our team

Technical Roles:
Systems Architect - takes lead on architecture design and oversees development is according to the blueprint

Frontend Engineer -
CI/CD Engineer - Focuses on creating workflows to support CI and CD

Research Roles:
Research Design Lead - Formulates the research questions, hypotheses, and experimental methodology for evaluating the user experience.
Ethics and Participant Coordinator - Drafts consent forms and helps recruit study participants
Data Collection Analyst - Builds logging mechanisms into the software to capture quantitative user metrics (time-played, score, etc.)
Qualitative Research Lead - Conducts/designs post-test interviews, surveys and focus groups
Statistical Data Analyst - Analyzes quantitative data to drive further decisions
Visualization Specialist - Takes all data collected and converts it into an easy to understand format
