\section*{Appendix --- Reflection}

\noindent \textbf{Reflection Questions Answered:}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Why is it important to create a development plan prior to starting the
    project?
    \item In your opinion, what are the advantages and disadvantages of using
    CI/CD?
    \item What disagreements did your group have in this deliverable, if any,
    and how did you resolve them?
\end{enumerate}

\noindent \textbf{Andreas:}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item A development plan is crucial to decide on various project details as 
	a team before work on the project begins. This helps to ensure that all 
	team members and project supervisors understand the specifics of project 
	planning (Git workflows, meeting planning, etc.), to avoid any development 
	risk incurred by delaying or failing to note important project details.
	\item CI/CD pipelines offer many advantages through automation, by 
	automating portions of many crucial development activities such as code 
	linting, unit testing, and pull request review when team members push their 
	code. A disadvantage of utilizing extensive CI/CD pipelines could be the 
	overhead work required to create and maintain the pipelines, so a balance 
	should be struck to ensure any CI/CD used assists development work as much 
	as possible, without requiring any high-effort maintenance.
	\item Our team had some small disagreements in different sections of the 
	document and aspects of the development plan, such as concerning project 
	planning / project workflow decisions, proof of concept ideas, and others. 
	As with any team disconnect on project decisions, our team reviewed the 
	decisions to be made together, collaboratively discussed the pros and 
	cons of each option, and reached a conclusion as a team.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent \textbf{Owen:}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Having a development plan before starting the project is crucial to really 
	map out where the project will go. This is especially important in a team of 
	people, since keeping everyone on the same page and planning logistics is the 
	highest priority, and much different to a solo project, or one of a lower scope. 
	Having a development plan, for the above reasons and more, help to avoid 
	typical pitfalls of development like scope creep or a bad workflow.
	\item CI/CD pipelines are extremely advantageous to use for a project, since it 
	can help automate portions of the development cycle and make iterations easier. 
	The initial setup of the pipelines and the potential upkeep are both potential 
	disadvantages since they would take more time, but the opposite argument 
	could also be made. This argument is that if enough care and effort is put into 
	creating the pipelines, then through the setup process the team would grow to 
	understand their design process and goals more during this process.
	\item In this deliverable our team disagreed on some small aspects, like idea 
	specifics, wording, or design decisions. Resolutions were quite straightforward, 
	since everyone communicates quite well so far. This makes it easy to review and 	
	discuss decisions while ensuring everyone stays informed, since discussions were 
	civil and productive, reaching verdicts where everyone has had input.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent \textbf{Humna:}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item A development plan is crucial prior to starting the project as it allows for overall team 
	organization and management, logistical details, expected technologies, workflow planning, and 
	proof of concept planning. All of these factors are important to discuss as a group before starting 
	the implementation stage to ensure that the team members are on the same page. Through this deliverable, 
	our team specified a meeting schedule, the technologies we expect to use, how we will collaborate through 
	our workflow plan, and our understanding of the proof of concept. As a team, we also set expectations on 
	AI usage and held open discussions on our expectations.
	\item There are many advantages of using CI/CD. It can allow for smoother progression as we develop our software 
	by automatically checking changes and helping identify issues before they are merged into the main branch. It can 
	also help make collaboration between team members more organized. However, one limitation to this approach is that 
	it can take more time to set up and maintain, and workflow issues can take time to troubleshoot.
	\item There were no major disagreements during the deliverable. Our group shared a similar vision of what we wanted to 
	achieve from this project. Conversations were held regarding the team's openness and willingness to explore new technologies, 
	and because we are in the same program, we generally use technologies such as GitHub and Git similarly.
\end{enumerate}
