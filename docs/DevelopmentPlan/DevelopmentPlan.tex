\section*{Appendix --- Reflection}

\noindent \textbf{Reflection Questions Answered:}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Why is it important to create a development plan prior to starting the
    project?
    \item In your opinion, what are the advantages and disadvantages of using
    CI/CD?
    \item What disagreements did your group have in this deliverable, if any,
    and how did you resolve them?
\end{enumerate}

\noindent \textbf{Andreas:}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item A development plan is crucial to decide on various project details as 
	a team before work on the project begins. This helps to ensure that all 
	team members and project supervisors understand the specifics of project 
	planning (Git workflows, meeting planning, etc.), to avoid any development 
	risk incurred by delaying or failing to note important project details.
	\item CI/CD pipelines offer many advantages through automation, by 
	automating portions of many crucial development activities such as code 
	linting, unit testing, and pull request review when team members push their 
	code. A disadvantage of utilizing extensive CI/CD pipelines could be the 
	overhead work required to create and maintain the pipelines, so a balance 
	should be struck to ensure any CI/CD used assists development work as much 
	as possible, without requiring any high-effort maintenance.
	\item Our team had some small disagreements in different sections of the 
	document and aspects of the development plan, such as concerning project 
	planning / project workflow decisions, proof of concept ideas, and others. 
	As with any team disconnect on project decisions, our team reviewed the 
	decisions to be made together, collaboratively discussed the pros and 
	cons of each option, and reached a conclusion as a team.
\end{enumerate}
