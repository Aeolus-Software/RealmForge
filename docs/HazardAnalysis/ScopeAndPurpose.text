The scope of this document is to provide an analysis of the system as it pertains to potential hazards. The analysis will identify hazards present in the components/boundaries of the system, provide mitigation strategies for each hazard, and provide the resulting safety and security requirements. 
\newline
\newline
This analysis is important, as certain losses can be incurred by the hazards if left unchecked, such as:

\begin{itemize}

	\item Lower player count and interest

	\item Player frustration

	\item Unintended behavior, such as bugs, lag, or crashes

	\item Lack of usability testing data to work from

\end{itemize}
