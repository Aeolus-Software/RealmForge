The successful development and deployment of RealmForge will yield several key benefits designed to resolve the current shortcomings in independent game \hyperlink{RogueliteDef}{roguelite} design and usability evaluation:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A Complete Core Gameplay Loop:} RealmForge will deliver a fully playable \hyperlink{RogueliteDef}{roguelite} experience featuring a central hub, randomized or branched area traversal, dynamic enemy encounters, and persistent resource management that rewards players across multiple runs.
\item \textbf{Empirical Usability Framework:} Provide a framework to allow developers to easily extract player data from testing to drive future development.
\item \textbf{Configurable Design Flexibility:} The system will provide explicit runtime feature variation capabilities, allowing developers and researchers to toggle interface layouts, combat balancing, and mechanical behaviors on the fly without recompiling the executable.
\item \textbf{Objective Data-Driven Insights:} Moving away from subjective intuition, RealmForge will equip designers with concrete statistical data to accurately evaluate player engagement, identify pacing bottlenecks, and determine which mechanical variations genuinely optimize the user experience.
\end{itemize}
