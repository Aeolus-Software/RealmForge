\requirement{ENV}
{The system shall be compatible with user machines running Windows 10 or 11 
(64-bit) operating systems that meet the following recommended specifications:
\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{CPU:} x86\_64 \hyperlink{CPU}{CPU} with 
	\hyperlink{SSE4}{SSE4.2} support, with 
	4 physical cores 
	or more. Example: Intel Core i5-6600K, \hyperlink{AMD}{AMD} Ryzen 5 1600.
	\item \textbf{GPU:} Dedicated graphics \hyperlink{GPU}{GPU} with full 
	\hyperlink{OpenGL}{OpenGL} 4.6 support. 
	Example: \hyperlink{NVIDIA}{NVIDIA} GeForce GTX 650, AMD Radeon HD 7750 
	(\hyperlink{GCN}{GCN} 1.0).
	\item \textbf{RAM:} A minimum of 8 GB of \hyperlink{RAM}{RAM}.
\end{itemize}}
{This environment constraint is imposed to focus development of RealmForge on 
the most commonly used operating system for \hyperlink{PC}{PC} video games, 
Windows. The system may be extended to support other operating systems in the 
future if possible. The recommended specifications are included to ensure user 
machines can fully execute the system, accompanying any future decisions to 
extend its capabilities.}
{The system can be compiled and executed on machines running Windows 10 or 11 
(64-bit) operating systems, meeting the recommended specifications outlined 
above, at a stable 60 \hyperlink{FPS}{fps}.}

\requirement{ENV}
{The system shall provide user input capabilities for keyboard and mouse 
peripherals, as well as common controllers (PlayStation, Xbox).}
{The system must accommodate the use of various input methods (keyboard and 
mouse, different controllers) to facilitate usability studies incorporating 
these varying input methods.}
{The system can receive all game inputs required for its successful operation 
from the following input methods: USB Keyboard and Mouse, Xbox Series X/S, Xbox 
One, or Xbox 360 controllers, and PlayStation DualSense or DualShock 4 
controllers.}
