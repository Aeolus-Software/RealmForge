Currently, independent game studios look toward the \hyperlink{RogueliteDef}{roguelite} genre due to its huge success in recent years as well as a realistic way for smaller studios to replicate the massive amounts of content larger studios are able to create. \hyperlink{RogueliteDef}{Roguelites} allow for independent game developers to circumvent the limitations caused by fewer resources compared to AAA studios by prioritizing the replayability of the game through randomized runs and how different mechanics can interact with each other to create entirely new experiences.
\newline
\newline
Despite these architectural advantages, the development process of these independent game projects often have shortcomings due to relying on intuition, and poor, unstructured playtesting. Often, independant developers make critical mistakes while balancing the game, for instance, testing it only with their friends and family who provide biased feedback and do not meet characteristics of all target audiences. Games are also frequently tested too late in the development process when it is too late to make significant changes. Furthermore, developers often do not have objective metrics, relying solely on the subjective impressions received from play testers. Developers do not have reliable statistical data on what mechanics and interface elements engage the players for the longest time.
