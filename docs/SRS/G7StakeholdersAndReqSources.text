\noindent
The list of stakeholders, along with their relevancy to the project, are as follows:


\begin{itemize}

	\item Capstone course facilitators: This group oversees development, and gives important advice which informs many important project decisions, particularly to do with logistics and scope.

	\item People who have game development experience: This group can give perspective/feedback about technical aspects of game development in a unique way from other stakeholders.

	\item People who play video games and are experienced with the roguelike/roguelite genre: This group would be the main demographic the game is catered to, and would have feedback which is most relevant to the experience as it pertains to the genre.

	\item People who play video games but are not experienced with the roguelike/roguelite genre: This group would be important to determine the approachability of the genre as a whole.

	\item People who have little to no experience with video games: This group would be important to the usability testing, and inform requirements to have variations in the game be more accessible.

\end{itemize}
