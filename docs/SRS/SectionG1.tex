\subsection*{Context and Overall Objectives}
In recent years, the popularity of games in the roguelite genre has massively increased as seen in large titles such as \textit{Hades}, \textit{Slay the Spire}, \textit{Balatro}, and more. The surge in popularity of these types of games has caused the development of many new projects aiming to follow in the footsteps of these major successes; however, in an attempt to quickly recreate their success, they generate poorly designed games without unique or engaging mechanics which often stems from failing to conduct effective, quantifiable usability testing.
\newline
Aeolus Software presents RealmForge, our own unique take on the roguelite genre. We aim to create a game which is built upon a modular base, allowing us to easily vary core UI and gameplay mechanics. Through the multitude of variations of our game we will produce, we will conduct extensive testing on different features and designs to determine what provides the best overall experience for our users.
