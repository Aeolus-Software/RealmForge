The area of testing below will focus on on the configurations aspect of RealmForge.
The configurations are important because they control the overall dynamic experience
of the game. Ensuring they work is a key to properly allow users to experiment, test, and provide
feed for usability.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \functest{Config-Test-Health}
    {Configuration Test Health}
    {Manual, Dynamic}
    {The base increase for player health is +/- 0}
    {Change in the range control of the configuration health option}
    {The displayed health change is equal to the increment increase of the range control}
    {The expected output is justified based on the SFR-006 in the SRS document}
    {\begin{enumerate} 
        \item Enter the configuration menu
        \item Select the player health option
        \item Raise / Lower value via control dial
    \end{enumerate}} 
\end{enumerate}
