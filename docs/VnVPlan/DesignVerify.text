The approaches listed below will be used to verify the project's software 
design 
document 
(once it is completed for each revision) with respect to course standards, 
checklists, 
the 
document's implementation feasibility, and its effectiveness in providing a 
design that satisfies the SRS's requirements (among other relevant qualities).  
These methods are 
selected to provide confidence in the document, while also being feasible in 
the 
context of the project (as mentioned similarly for the verification of the 
SRS).\newline

\noindent \textbf{Verification Approaches:}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Course Peer Review and Update Tracking:} The design document's 
initial revision (Revision -1) will 
reviewed by another Capstone team, against the rubric and success criteria 
provided by the course. Suggestions and feedback provided by this peer 
review are tracked and maintained by GitHub issues, and suitable changes to 
address the feedback provided will be implemented before the completion of the 
document's next two revisions (0 and 1). The team that conducted the peer 
review of the 
document may be contacted to further clarify their feedback, to ensure 
necessary changes are made.
\item \textbf{Structured Team Design Document Reviews:} Prior to the completion 
of the software design document for each of its revisions (-1, 0, and 1), the 
RealmForge development team 
will conduct a structured review of the document, considering various 
qualities it must exhibit (as discussed in course lectures, rubrics, and 
checklists focused on the design document). These reviews will involve live 
team 
meetings, 
where team members will walk through each section of the document and 
verify its contents against the provided course design document checklist, and 
the document's associated rubric. Prior to the completion of the document for 
its final two revisions (0 and 1), these reviews will be conducted following 
all required 
document updates, such as those motivated by peer review feedback, TA 
feedback, course instructor feedback, or supervisor feedback. Any further 
required updates found during each review will be tracked and maintained in 
the team's GitHub repository and project (using issues).
\end{itemize}
