All members will contribute to and complete the
verification and validation of RealmForge. Each team member has been
assigned specific sections of the VnV Plan and is responsible for
developing system tests related to their assigned areas, such as 
configuration and physical controls, combat, central hub functionality,
overall gameplay and map navigation, and user interfaces.

Team members will also be assigned responsibility to review sections
completed by other team members to ensure that tests align with the
project requirements that have been outlined in the SRS documentation.
