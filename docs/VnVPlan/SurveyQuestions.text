The following questions will be posed to participants of the RealmForge 
software's primary system playtests, as outlined in 
\ref{UISNFTmainplaytest}:\newline

\noindent \textbf{Primary System Playtest Survey:}

\begin{enumerate}
\item During your gameplay session, which configurations of the player 
character's starting health were tested?
\item How was your experience impacted by varying this feature's parameters? 
Did increasing the player character's initial health cause the game to be more 
engaging and rewarding to play, or the opposite? How did reducing the player 
character's initial health impact your experience?
\item Which configuration of this feature that you tested resulted in the most 
engaging experience, in your opinion?
\item During your gameplay session, which configurations of the rewards gained 
following an enemy encounter were tested?
\item How was your experience impacted by varying this feature's parameters? 
Did increasing the rewards granted to you following a successful enemy 
encounter cause the game to be more engaging and rewarding to play, or the 
opposite? How did reducing the rewards granted impact your experience?
\item Which configuration of this feature that you tested resulted in the most 
engaging experience, in your opinion?
\item During your gameplay session, which configurations of the central hub's 
upgrade costs were tested?
\item How was your experience impacted by varying this feature's parameters? 
Did increasing the costs of upgrades available in the central hub cause the 
game to be more engaging and rewarding to play, or the opposite? How did 
reducing the costs impact your experience?
\item Which configuration of this feature that you tested resulted in the most 
engaging experience, in your opinion?
\item During your gameplay session, which configurations of the enemy encounter 
combat pace were tested?
\item How was your experience impacted by varying this feature's parameters? 
Did increasing the speed of enemy encounters cause the game to be more engaging 
and rewarding to play, or the opposite? How did reducing the speed impact your 
experience?
\item Which configuration of this feature that you tested resulted in the most 
engaging experience, in your opinion?
\item Did you encounter any errors, unexpected events, or unexpected displayed 
visuals during your game session? If so:
	\begin{enumerate}
	\item What occurred?
	\item Which part of the game were you playing at the time?
	\item What inputs did you provide?
	\end{enumerate}
\item Did any features of the game negatively impact your experience, or were 
any features frustrating (for example, if a certain menu was difficult to use)? 
If so, which features, and why?
\item Do you have any suggestions for new features that would improve your 
experience when playing the game, or modifications to existing features that 
would improve your experience?
\end{enumerate}
