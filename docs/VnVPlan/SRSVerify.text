The following approaches will be used to verify the software requirements 
specification for completeness, correctness, verifiability, avoidance of 
ambiguity, and traceability, among other relevant attributes:

\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Course Peer Review and Update Tracking:} The SRS document was 
	reviewed by another Capstone team, against the rubric and success criteria 
	provided by the course. Suggestions and feedback provided by this peer 
	review are tracked and maintained by GitHub issues, and suitable changes to 
	address the feedback provided will be implemented before the document's 
	Revision 1 completion. The team that conducted the peer review of the 
	document may be contacted to further clarify their feedback, to ensure 
	necessary changes are made.
	\item \textbf{Structured Team Requirements Review:} Prior to the completion 
	of the SRS for the project's Revision 1, the RealmForge development team 
	will conduct a structured review of the document, considering various 
	qualities it must exhibit (completeness, correctness, etc. as listed above 
	and in the course SRS checklist). This review will be a live team meeting, 
	where team members will walk through each section of the document and 
	verify its contents against the provided course SRS checklist, and the SRS 
	document's rubric. This review will be conducted following all required 
	document updates, such as those motivated by peer review feedback, TA 
	feedback, course instructor feedback, or supervisor feedback. Any further 
	required updates found during the review will be tracked and maintained in 
	the team's GitHub repository and project (using issues).
\end{itemize}
