\subsection{How and Where Used}
Microsft Co-pilot was used for LaTeX debugging and generating of small code snippets to help enhance the template building process. On occasions whenever there was an unknown LaTeX error,
Co-pilot was used to give suggestions or error analysis to unknown the 
problem.\\

Google AI Overview and Claude were used for assistance with LaTeX syntax and 
commands, during 
the development of the Commonalities section, formatting of the revision 
history table, as well as other various small 
formatting 
fixes. In these sections, 
AI tools were not used to generate any content text, or wholly generate any of 
the 
ideas or conclusions presented.

\subsection{Verification of AI-Generated Content}

Any LaTeX syntax or code provided by AI tools was verified by testing 
compilation in LaTeX documents, and ensuring the resulting output was 
correctly formatted and matched the needs of the document. No ideas or text 
were 
newly generated by AI tools and presented in the document.
