\section*{Appendix --- Reflection}

\noindent \textbf{Reflection Questions Answered:}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item What went well while writing this deliverable?
    \item What pain points did you experience during this deliverable, and how
    did you resolve them?
    \item How did you and your team adjust the scope of your goals to ensure
    they are suitable for a Capstone project (not overly ambitious but also of
    appropriate complexity for a senior design project)?
\end{enumerate}

\noindent \textbf{Andreas:}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item While writing the deliverable, our team collaborated very well, with 
	all team members evenly dividing the work to be done, communicating their 
	progress on each task, and completing their work in time. Team members 
	would frequently update the team on their progress on work tasks, and would 
	often collaborate with other members to make larger project decisions in 
	our meetings and Microsoft Teams chat. Our team also regularly communicated 
	with our professor and TAs to clarify any questions we had regarding each 
	part of the deliverable, helping to ensure it was completed to the best of 
	our ability.
	\item One pain point I had individually during this deliverable was at 
	times being unsure about the exact requirements for each document section, 
	and experiencing perfectionism when writing my document sections. Initially 
	just completing each section from the information available in the document 
	template, I found that I could also rely on sample documents, the course 
	rubrics, and course lectures to clarify uncertainties on the requirements 
	for each document section, assisting me in completing each section to the 
	best of my ability. I also had issues with being unable to finalize my 
	sections due to a wish to perfect each one, but I was able to resolve this 
	by completing reviews of my own work, and trusting my teammates to find any 
	potential errors in their reviews.
	\item Our team adjusted the scope of our goals many times while creating 
	our project idea. Initially planning to just develop a video game, 
	reviewing this concept with Dr.~Smith lead us to decide to add further 
	complexity to ensure the project would be suitable for Capstone. This was 
	done by adding the aspect of incorporating explicit source code variations 
	in the game, to facilitate more effective usability testing. The project 
	was also further scoped by all team members discussing what would be 
	possible for the game's features, and deciding on a minimal, possible 
	option that could be extended if extra time is available.
\end{enumerate} 

\noindent \textbf{Owen:}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item While writing this deliverable, many things went well. Work was divided 
	efficiently, group members checked in with their progress and checked in on 
	others, and they would ask for help/collaboration when necessary. This led to 
	important decisions being reviewed by more people. While writing, the team 
	was also continuously talking with our contacts, like our Capstone supervisor, 
	course instructor, and TA to ask questions and receive continuous 
	advice/feedback when writing and reviewing the document. This was done both 
	virtually and in-person, which is important since in-person correspondence is a 
	nice resource to have.
	\item There were two pain points I can think of for this deliverable. First, I had a 
	slight mental block when asking the group for feedback, since the project is still 
	in its very early stages. I feel like communicating and asking for help when 
	needed is something I need to work on, and I am resolving this by trusting both 
	my group and myself to produce quailty work, which will also get easier as the 
	group works together for longer. The second pain point was with course 
	navigation. This pain point was resolved as I found more resources, such as the 
	rubrics in Avenue to Learn, which were found inside a menu that no other 
	course I've been in has made use of.
	\item Our team discussed the scope of the project for a while to ensure the 
	goals are suitable. Using a project proposed from a potential supervisor and 
	modifying it from there was certainly a good start. The team was able to 
	workshop the modified idea with Dr. Welch to ensure he was on board to 
	supervise, while receiving input from Dr. Smith on the feasibility and viability 
	of the project. Feedback from Dr. Smith turned the project idea more towards 	
	the goal of usability testing, which we then iterated further with Dr. Welch, with 
	whom we discussed new goals/stretch goals to facilitate this change (like usability 
	testing simulations). Finally, all members of the team played integral roles in 
	scoping the project throughout this process by discussing and providing insight 
	into each other's strengths, weaknesses, and technical proficiencies.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent \textbf{Humna:}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item This deliverable helped me solidify the project our group aims to pursue. In the earlier 
	brainstorming stages, our group faced doubts and discussions surrounding the project. This deliverable 
	helped scope the project down to its core components, understand the main goals we wish to achieve 
	throughout the capstone course, and identify the scope we hope to pursue, including our usability study. 
	The deliverable helped me understand the bigger ``why'' behind the project and define it in a clear way. 
	Prior to this deliverable, there was uncertainty about what the project was, how it would come to life, and how 
	we would begin approaching it. By defining the project, it made it feel more tangible and something that can actually be tackled. 
	\item Our group spent the beginning weeks of capstone solidifying our idea and clearing any existing doubts. Through 
	discussions with our supervisor and group meetings, we developed a clear vision of where the project was headed. Many of 
	these concerns were addressed before completing the deliverable; hence, no major pain points were experienced. For example, 
	we ensured usability studies were included in the project to meet the project requirements, and we finalized key aspects of 
	how the game would work, such as having the central hub play a critical role. It also helped me understand the importance of 
	clear communication moving forward to ensure all members continue to share a unified vision of the project.
	\item Our team adjusted the goals to ensure that usability studies played a major role in the project. The scope of our 
	usability study was expanded, and different methods were considered, such as the possibility of utilizing simulated 
	gameplay. The group also decided to implement variations in game features to allow for a more exhaustive range of 
	testing. This helped ensure the project was appropriate for the capstone course based on the project requirements 
	and our discussions with Dr. Smith.
\end{enumerate}
