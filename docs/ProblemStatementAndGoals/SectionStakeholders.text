Our stakeholders will fall into 5 distinct categories: 

\begin{itemize}

    \item Capstone course facilitators who give important advice which informs many important project decisions.

    \item People who have game development experience and can give unique perspectives/feedback

    \item People who play video games and are experienced with the roguelike/roguelite genre

    \item People who play video games but are not experienced with the roguelike/roguelite genre

    \item People who have little to no experience with video games

\end{itemize}

\noindent These stakeholders will be found both on and off the McMaster campus. Some potential examples of stakeholders include:

\begin{itemize}

    \item Friends and family

    \item Classmates

    \item Members of McMaster clubs/teams

\end{itemize}
