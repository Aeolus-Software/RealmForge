The stretch goals of this project include:

\begin{enumerate}

    \item Develop a gameplay simulator that can test variations of game features, enabling comparisons between them and assessing their effects on gameplay diversity and gameplay behaviour.

    \item Expand the roguelite game by adding additional encounters or mechanics to increase gameplay variety and overall complexity for the player.

    \item Compare results from simulated gameplay and human gameplay to identify differences and limitations between the two approaches and strengthen conclusions from the conducted usability studies.

    \item Expand the types of variations being tested, including gameplay, interface features, and control methods, to cover more factors that could affect the player experience and make the usability study more comprehensive.

\end{enumerate}
