## Project Description:
RealmForge is a roguelite adventure game, where players explore and battle through a world of turn-based enemy encounters. During each encounter, the player character will battle enemies by selecting various actions. Each action the player can use depends on certain resources, provided by a central hub, which is integral to the gameplay loop. While playing the game, the player is able to upgrade the central hub to improve its abilities to generate resources. Upgrading the central hub can also modify the player’s available actions to improve their effectiveness. After succeeding in an encounter, the player is rewarded with a currency that is used to upgrade the hub. Various encounters available to the player will reward them with different types of currency that can be used in different ways. When the player fails at some point in their adventure, they are returned to the beginning, and their hub and resources are reset. However, major upgrades to the hub can be retained through different ‘runs’ of the game.

When playing RealmForge, players will move through a path of connected locations, with each location requiring the player to overcome a single encounter with enemies. Players will manage resources produced by their central hub to battle enemies during their turn, after which the enemies will have a chance to fight back. These encounters will mainly involve the player navigating through various menus to complete their turn, selecting different actions (such as various attacks, defensive maneuvers, etc.). During each encounter, players will need to strategize to effectively use the resources provided by the hub, and strategically select upgrades that will assist them during their adventure.

To improve RealmForge’s user experience, the game will be developed to incorporate variations in different features (at run time), to facilitate usability testing. Different users will be able to play the game with varying features, and provide feedback on their experience. For example, one build of the game could involve upgrades to the central hub providing the player with new attacks to use and more efficient resource production, while another could instead provide the player with mathematically-based modifiers to their existing attacks, allowing them to systematically scale their character’s power.
